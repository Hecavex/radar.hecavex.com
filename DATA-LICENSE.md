The HECAVEX label can mean an observation from the optional sanitized HTTPS export or an explicit sanitized candidate accepted through the local review workflow; the controlled `discoveredVia` field distinguishes `hecavex-public-export` from `hecavex-review`. HECAVEX operators remain responsible for the current access, attribution, redistribution, and rate-limit terms of every enabled service input.

The observation-only static STIX 2.1 distribution is an unmarked format projection of the current public Radar signal set. The separate reviewed Indicator distribution applies the standard TLP:CLEAR marking to explicit, bounded analyst confirmations. Neither the marking nor either feed's use of standard STIX objects, deterministic identifiers, or raw interoperable domain-name values relicenses an observation, waives a source's terms, or grants permission to redistribute third-party report metadata. Consumers must preserve applicable source attribution and rights. Inclusion is not actor attribution or a blocking recommendation.

## Source-specific notes

- **Certificate Transparency / CertStream:** certificate observations are public CT facts. The Radar matcher and collector are Apache-2.0. The scheduled workflow runs [`reloading01/certstream-server-rust`](https://github.com/reloading01/certstream-server-rust), which is separately distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/reloading01/certstream-server-rust/blob/main/LICENSE); CT-log software and services retain their own terms.
- **URLScan:** report metadata, screenshots, and hashes remain subject to URLScan's terms and depicted-site rights. Before enabling or changing URLScan publication on the live service, HECAVEX must confirm that the account and plan permit the intended automated access, display, and redistribution; an API key alone does not grant those rights.
- **[PhishDestroy Primary Active](https://github.com/phishdestroy/destroylist):** used only as a transient discovery seed under its [MIT license](https://github.com/phishdestroy/destroylist/blob/main/LICENSE). Copyright (c) 2019 PhishDestroy. Raw list rows are not copied; a domain may appear later only after independent URLScan validation.
- **[CERT Polska Warning List](https://hole.cert.pl/):** used only as a transient discovery seed under the processing permission stated in its [public API specification](https://hole.cert.pl/schema/certpl_lista_ostrzezen_api_v2.pdf). Raw list rows are not copied; a domain may appear later only after independent URLScan validation.
- **[Phishing.Database ACTIVE domains](https://github.com/Phishing-Database/Phishing.Database):** used only as a transient discovery seed under its [MIT license](https://github.com/Phishing-Database/Phishing.Database/blob/master/LICENSE). Raw list rows are not copied; a domain may appear later only after independent URLScan validation.
- **[hagezi NRD](https://github.com/hagezi/nrd) (newly registered domains, last 7 days):** licensed under [GPL-3.0](https://github.com/hagezi/nrd/blob/main/LICENSE). Radar downloads the list during a URLScan hunt, matches it in memory against the reviewed registry and keeps only matching names as transient search hints for that run. No list rows are copied into `data/`, archives or published files, so the list is not redistributed; a domain may appear later only after independent URLScan validation.
- **HECAVEX inputs:** observations may enter through an explicitly configured sanitized public export or an intentional sanitized local review export. Every row must pass the current public matcher and publication validation. The operator remains responsible for excluding private history, proprietary evidence, credentials, personal data, and material it cannot publish.
- **Screenshots:** remain subject to URLScan's terms and depicted-site rights. The publisher accepts screenshots only from HTTPS on exactly `urlscan.io`, never a subdomain.
- **Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal:** optional maintainer checks are not publication sources. Public Actions can perform a bounded VirusTotal request for one existing signal but deliberately emits no provider result. Google Safe Browsing is local-only because its v5 response requires a private per-URL expiry cache, including for no-match results. Results are not added to the dataset, public summaries, logs, artifacts, feeds, scoring, or suppression logic. Provider terms, attribution requirements, quotas, and commercial-use limits remain controlling.

Brand names and domains in `data/brands-lt.json` identify their owners and do not imply endorsement. Each registry entry must cite an authoritative public source.
